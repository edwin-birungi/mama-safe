First time here? How to use this dashboard
  1. Keep this page openAlerts appear on their own every few seconds. Turn on Sound alert so a beep plays when a new emergency arrives (your browser needs one click first).
  2. Choose your facilityUse the Facility menu to see only the alerts sent to your hospital or clinic.
  3. Read the alertEach red card shows the mother's name and phone (tap to call), her week of pregnancy, her symptoms, the channel she used (app, USSD or SMS), a link to her location when her phone shared it, and her emergency contact.
  4. Respond, then press "I am acting on this"This marks the alert acknowledged, turns it green and records who and when. You can also reply ACK <alert number> by SMS from the facility's alert mobile.
  5. Watch for the red warningIf a card says The facility was NOT sent an SMS, nobody was told by text. Phone the facility or the mother straight away.

Transport tab: the drivers who can be asked to collect a mother. Alerts show who was asked and who accepted. Keep numbers current: a wrong number means nobody comes.

Questions tab: questions mothers could not get answered in the app. Call or text her back, then press Done. Each one also arrives by SMS on the facility alert phone.

Messages tab: every SMS the system sent or tried to send, with its status. Use it to check that reminders and alerts really went out.

An alert nobody reads is dangerous. Decide who watches this page and the alert phone in every shift, and what happens if an alert is not acknowledged. Escalation is not automatic.

Emergency transport directory

Ambulances, boda riders, taxi drivers and volunteers who agreed to be contacted. When a mother sends an alert, the nearest ones get an SMS and reply GO <alert number>; the first to reply gets her number. They can text BUSY or AVAILABLE at any time. Only add people who agreed, and use a mobile number that receives SMS. Drivers without a GPS base are only found by district.
